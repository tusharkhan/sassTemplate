Getting started

About ten minutes if you already have an API key. Install the SDK, create a room server-side, mint a publisher token and call joinRoom in the browser. The quickstart in our documentation is three snippets long and there is no signalling server for you to run.

Only your own application backend, and only to keep your secret key off the client. Everything else — ingest, transcoding, delivery, recording, the AI pipeline — runs on our side. Many customers ship with a single serverless function that mints tokens.

Every evergreen browser on desktop and mobile: Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari, plus WebViews on iOS 16+ and Android API 26+. Native SDKs exist for Swift and Kotlin when you want camera control the web cannot give you.

Yes. The free tier includes 1,000 streaming minutes a month, the full global network and every endpoint, and it does not expire. We ask for a card only when you cross into a paid plan.

Streaming & quality

Median glass-to-glass latency across the fleet is 47ms, with the 95th percentile at 91ms. Those are measured end to end by synthetic publishers and subscribers in every region, not from the moment a frame enters our servers. Live figures are on the status page.

There is no architectural ceiling — delivery is pull-based from the edge, so viewers scale independently of the publisher. The largest single broadcast we have carried peaked at 1.4 million concurrent viewers. Your plan sets a soft limit you can raise by asking.

The jitter buffer adapts per connection, targeting one frame of headroom above observed p99 jitter, and the bitrate ladder drops resolution before it drops frames. On genuinely poor connections the buffer grows — viewers get slightly more delay instead of a stall.

Yes. Publish RTMP or SRT to ingest.wavenode.io with your stream key. WebRTC gets you the lowest latency, but RTMP is fully supported and is what most hardware encoders speak.

AI features

Word error rate is typically 4–7% on clear audio in a supported language, which is competitive with offline transcription. Accuracy on proper nouns improves substantially if you upload a custom vocabulary — product names and rosters are where generic models struggle.

Captions in 42 languages and real-time translation between any supported pair. The free tier includes five languages; paid plans include all of them. The current list is in the documentation and grows a few times a year.

The models look for laughter, applause, sustained chat activity and topic changes, then cut around the detected moment with a configurable lead-in. You get candidates with confidence scores rather than a single answer, so you can filter or let creators pick.

No. Customer media is never used for training, by default or by opt-in. Recordings and transcripts exist only to serve your API calls and are deleted on the retention schedule you set.

Billing

Yes. Set a hard monthly ceiling per project in the dashboard and choose whether to throttle or keep streaming when it is reached. Alerts fire at 50%, 80% and 100% over webhook and email, so nobody finds out from the invoice.

Yes, and they are metered independently — you are charged for what you keep as well as what you send. Recordings move to a colder tier automatically as they age, and pointing us at your own S3 bucket removes our storage line from the invoice entirely.

They stay downloadable through the API for 30 days after the account closes, then they are deleted along with the backups. Export before you go, or ask support to extend the window — we would rather you left with your media than without it.

Prices exclude VAT and sales tax, which are added at checkout based on your billing country. Every invoice carries your VAT registration number, a purchase-order reference if you supply one, and is downloadable as both PDF and CSV.

Security & compliance

SOC 2 Type II, renewed annually, with the report available under NDA. The platform is GDPR-compliant and HIPAA-ready, and we sign BAAs on Business plans. Penetration test summaries are shared on request.

Yes. Business plans can pin every workload — ingest, processing, storage and AI inference — to the EU, US or APAC. Region-pinned ingest hostnames bypass anycast routing so media never leaves the jurisdiction.

Signed, expiring playback URLs by default, with optional DRM and per-room encryption keys. Room tokens are short-lived JWTs scoped to a single room and role, so a leaked token is useless within the hour.

Email security@wavenode.io rather than using the contact form. We acknowledge within one business day and run a paid disclosure programme.
