Privacy Policy

What we collect, why we collect it, where it is stored, how long we keep it and what you can ask us to do with it — written to be read rather than to be defensible.

1. Who we are

WaveNode Inc. operates the video and audio streaming platform described on this site. For the purposes of the GDPR we are a data controller for the account data of our own customers, and a data processor for the media and end-user data our customers send through the Service.

Our data protection officer can be reached at privacy@wavenode.io. Where we act as a processor, our Data Processing Addendum forms part of your contract with us and governs in the event of any conflict with this notice.

2. What we collect

We try to collect as little as the Service allows. In practice that falls into four categories.

  • Account data. Name, work email, company, billing address and payment method, plus the contents of any support conversation.
  • Usage and telemetry. API calls, streaming minutes, region, resolution, latency and error rates — the same metrics we show you in the dashboard.
  • Customer media. Video, audio, recordings, transcripts and generated clips, processed strictly on your instructions.
  • Website data. Page views and referrers, collected without cookies for advertising purposes.

3. How we use it

Account data is used to provide the Service, bill for it and support you. Usage data is used to operate the network, plan capacity, investigate incidents and calculate your invoice. Customer media is used only to perform the operations you request — delivery, recording, captioning, translation, clipping.

Our lawful bases are contract performance for providing and billing the Service, legitimate interests for security, fraud prevention and capacity planning, and consent for marketing email, which you can withdraw at any time.

We do not sell personal data, we do not share it with advertising networks, and we do not use customer media to train machine-learning models.

4. AI processing

Captions, translations, chapters, highlight clips and moderation flags are produced by models we operate ourselves, inside the region you have selected. Media is held in memory for the duration of processing and written to storage only where you have enabled recording.

Model outputs are probabilistic and should not be treated as an authoritative record of what was said. Where captions or transcripts are used for accessibility or compliance, we recommend human review.

5. Who we share it with

We use a small number of sub-processors, each under a data processing agreement and each listed with its role and location in our documentation. Categories include cloud hosting, payment processing, email delivery and error reporting.

We disclose data to authorities only where legally compelled, and we will notify you before doing so unless prohibited by law. We publish a transparency report annually.

6. Where data is stored

By default, account and usage data is stored in the European Union. Customer media is processed at the edge location nearest the publisher and stored in the region attached to your project.

Business plans can pin every workload — ingest, processing, storage and inference — to the EU, US or APAC. Transfers outside the EEA, where they occur, rely on the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses.

7. How long we keep it

You control retention for media. Recordings, transcripts and clips are deleted on the schedule you configure, and within 24 hours when deletion is requested manually; backups are purged within 30 days.

Account data is retained for the life of the account and for six years afterwards where tax or accounting law requires it. Aggregated, non-identifying usage statistics may be kept indefinitely.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live you may have the right to access, correct, export, restrict or delete your personal data, to object to processing based on legitimate interests, and to withdraw consent.

Most of these can be exercised directly from the dashboard. For anything else, write to privacy@wavenode.io and we will respond within 30 days. If you are unsatisfied you may complain to your local supervisory authority; ours is the Berlin Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information.

If you are an end user of a service built on WaveNode rather than our direct customer, contact that service first — they decide what is collected and we act on their instructions.

9. Security

Media is encrypted in transit and at rest. Access to production systems requires hardware-backed multi-factor authentication and is logged. We hold SOC 2 Type II certification, renewed annually, and run an external penetration test at least once a year.

We will notify affected customers of a personal data breach without undue delay and within 72 hours of becoming aware of it, along with what we know and what we are doing about it.

Vulnerability reports go to security@wavenode.io. We acknowledge within one business day and operate a paid disclosure programme.

10. Cookies and tracking

The dashboard uses a single first-party session cookie and browser local storage for your theme preference. Neither is used for advertising or shared with third parties.

This marketing site sets no tracking cookies. Aggregate page-view analytics are collected without cookies and without building a profile of individual visitors.

11. Changes to this notice

We will post any revision here with an updated date, and email account holders at least 30 days before a material change takes effect. Previous versions are available on request.

12. Contacting us

Questions about this document can go to legal@wavenode.io, or by post to WaveNode Inc., Chausseestraße 112, 10115 Berlin, Germany.

For anything operational — an outage, a bug, an integration question — use the contact form instead; it reaches someone faster.
