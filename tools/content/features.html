All platform capabilities

Live video streaming

Push RTMP or WebRTC and we handle transcoding, adaptive bitrate ladders and HLS delivery. Streams start in under two seconds and scale from ten viewers to a million without a config change.

Audio rooms & podcasts

Opus-encoded spatial audio with echo cancellation and noise suppression on by default. Record every speaker to a separate track so your editor never has to un-mix a conversation.

Meetings & webinars

Host up to 10,000 participants with active-speaker layouts, screen share, breakout rooms and raise-hand. Moderator controls are exposed as API calls, so you can build your own stage.

AI captions & translation

Live captions in 42 languages at roughly 300ms behind the speaker, with real-time translation on the same socket. Finished transcripts land in your webhook as soon as the stream ends.

Automatic highlight clips

Our models watch for laughter, applause, chat spikes and topic changes, then cut vertical clips sized for Shorts, Reels and TikTok. A three-hour stream yields shareable clips in about four minutes.

Monetisation built in

Sell subscriptions, pay-per-view tickets and tips without wiring up a payment stack. Payouts run in 38 currencies and tax handling is managed for you in every market we support.

Recording & on-demand

Every session can be recorded to your own S3 bucket or ours, then replayed instantly with chapter markers generated from the transcript. Storage tiers move cold archives down automatically.

Real-time analytics

Watch concurrency, rebuffer ratio, join time and geography update every second while you are live. Export the same metrics to Datadog, Grafana or your warehouse over the metrics API.

Enterprise-grade security

SOC 2 Type II, GDPR and HIPAA-ready infrastructure with signed playback URLs, DRM and per-room encryption keys. Pin any workload to the EU, US or APAC region for data residency.

Global delivery

210+ edge locations, one endpoint

You publish to ingest.wavenode.io and we route the stream to the closest healthy point of presence. Viewers pull from the edge nearest to them, so a stream produced in Dhaka reaches São Paulo without a perceptible pause.

  • Anycast ingest. A single hostname worldwide — no region picking, no failover logic in your app.
  • Adaptive bitrate by default. Six rendition ladders are generated per stream, from 240p to 4K HDR.
  • 99.99% uptime SLA. Backed by financial credits on every paid plan, measured per region.

AI toolkit

Post-production that finishes itself

The same models that caption your stream also chapter it, clip it and summarise it. Everything is available over the API the moment a session ends, so your editors start from a rough cut instead of a raw file.

Speaker diarisation
Labelled per-speaker transcripts, even when two guests talk over each other.
Filler-word removal
Strip “um”, “uh” and dead air from the audio track with one flag.
Show notes
A summary, chapter list and pull-quotes returned as structured JSON.
Content moderation
Flag unsafe audio or video mid-stream and fire a webhook to your moderators.
