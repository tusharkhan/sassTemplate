Built for the work

The tedious parts, automated

Streaming is the fun half. These are the jobs that eat the rest of your week.

Vertical clips, automatically

Laughter, applause and chat spikes get cut into 9:16 clips sized for Shorts, Reels and TikTok. A three-hour stream gives you postable clips in about four minutes.

Subscriptions, tickets and tips

Sell recurring access, one-off event tickets or accept tips mid-stream. A flat 3% platform fee, payouts in two business days, tax handled in every market we support.

Captions in 42 languages

Live captions roughly 300ms behind you, with translation on the same socket. Your audience stops being limited to the languages you speak.

Per-guest audio tracks

Every speaker is recorded to a separate track with noise suppression already applied, so your editor never has to un-mix a conversation again.

Show notes written for you

A summary, chapter markers and pull-quotes land in your inbox when the stream ends. Paste them straight into the description.

Same network as the enterprise

210+ edge locations and sub-50ms latency on every plan, including the free one. Nothing about delivery is held back for bigger customers.
