What Business adds

The requirements that block a rollout

Everything below is configuration, not a custom engagement. Nothing here needs a professional-services contract.

Data residency by region

Pin ingest, processing, storage and AI inference to the EU, US or APAC. Region-pinned hostnames bypass anycast routing so media never leaves the jurisdiction.

SSO, SCIM and audit logs

SAML via Okta or Entra ID, directory sync for joiners and leavers, and an immutable audit trail exportable to your SIEM.

Bring your own storage

Recordings written straight to your S3, R2, GCS or Azure bucket under a scoped role. We keep the manifest; you keep the media.

Meetings at 10,000 people

All-hands, webinars and town halls with active-speaker layouts, breakout rooms and moderator controls exposed as API calls.

Metrics in your own tools

Concurrency, rebuffer ratio, join time and buffer depth exported to Datadog, Grafana, OpenTelemetry or your warehouse.

Engineers in your Slack

A shared channel with a one-hour response target, staffed by people who can read the media pipeline rather than a ticket queue.
