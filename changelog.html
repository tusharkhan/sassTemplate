Changelog

Everything we shipped, including the boring parts

Breaking changes get 90 days of notice and a migration guide. Deprecations are listed here before they are enforced, never after.

Release history

v3.14 Latest 18 August 2026

Custom vocabulary for AI captions

Caption accuracy on product names, drug names and player rosters was the single most common support topic this year. You can now upload up to 5,000 terms per project and weight them.

Added

  • POST /v3/projects/:id/vocabulary accepts a term list with per-term boost values.
  • Recording manifests now include a speakerCount field.
  • Two new edge locations: Lagos and Lima.

Fixed

  • Screen-share tracks published from Safari 18 occasionally reported a zero frame rate for the first two seconds.
  • Webhook retries could deliver out of order after a 24-hour backoff. Ordering is now guaranteed per room.
v3.13 30 July 2026

Adaptive jitter buffering, on by default

The buffer work described in our latency write-up is now the default for every new room. Median glass-to-glass latency across the fleet now sits at 47ms, and rebuffer ratio improved rather than regressed.

Changed

  • New rooms default to buffer.mode: 'adaptive'. Existing rooms are unchanged until you opt in.
  • The analytics API exposes bufferDepthMs as a time series.

Read the engineering write-up →

v3.12 Deprecation 9 July 2026

Per-participant recording tracks

Composed MP4s and isolated per-speaker tracks are now produced in parallel rather than sequentially, so a three-hour session finishes post-processing in about four minutes instead of twenty.

Deprecated

  • recording.splitTracks is replaced by recording.outputs. The old field keeps working until 9 October 2026.
v3.11 16 June 2026

Anycast ingest for every region

A single publish hostname worldwide. You no longer pick a region or implement failover: ingest.wavenode.io routes to the nearest healthy point of presence and drains gracefully during maintenance.

Added

  • Region-pinned ingest hostnames remain available for data-residency workloads.
  • Kotlin SDK 2.0, with Compose helpers.
v3.10 21 May 2026

Spend caps and budget alerts

Set a hard monthly ceiling per project and choose whether to throttle or keep streaming when it is reached. Alerts fire at 50%, 80% and 100% over webhook and email.

Added

  • Usage dashboard breaks spend down by room, region and feature.
  • Invoices are downloadable as CSV alongside the PDF.

Never miss a breaking change

Deprecations are announced here and by email 90 days before enforcement. Subscribe using the form in the footer, or follow the RSS feed from your reader.
