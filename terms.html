1. Acceptance of these terms

These terms govern your access to the WaveNode platform, including the API, client SDKs, dashboard and documentation (together, the “Service”). By creating an account or issuing an API request you agree to them on behalf of yourself and any organisation you represent.

If you are entering into these terms for a company, you confirm that you have the authority to bind that company. Where a signed enterprise agreement exists between us, that agreement takes precedence over anything written here.

2. Accounts and API keys

You are responsible for everything that happens under your account, including usage by your own end users. Secret keys must be kept server-side and never shipped to a browser or mobile binary.

Notify us promptly if you believe a key has been exposed. Keys can be rotated from the dashboard at any time; a rotated key remains valid for one hour so that live sessions are not interrupted.

  • You must be at least 16 years old, or the age of digital consent in your jurisdiction, to hold an account.
  • One person or organisation may not hold multiple free-tier accounts to circumvent plan limits.
  • We may suspend an account that is being used to attack, probe or degrade the Service.

3. Acceptable use

The Service carries live media to real audiences, so misuse has immediate consequences for other customers. You agree not to use it for any of the following.

  • Content you do not hold the rights to distribute, including re-streaming third-party broadcasts.
  • Material that is unlawful in the jurisdictions where it will be received, or that depicts the sexual exploitation of minors.
  • Harassment, incitement to violence, or coordinated deceptive behaviour.
  • Attempts to circumvent metering, rate limits, spend caps or region pinning.
  • Reverse engineering the Service other than as permitted by applicable law.

We do not pre-screen content. Where a stream is reported or flagged by automated moderation, we may suspend that room while we review it, and we will tell you why.

4. Plans, usage and payment

Paid plans are billed in advance, monthly or annually, and metered usage above your plan allowance is billed in arrears at the rates published on our pricing page. Prices exclude VAT and any other applicable tax.

Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated. Downgrades take effect at the start of the next billing period. Invoices are due on receipt unless a net-30 arrangement has been agreed in writing.

If an invoice is more than 14 days overdue we may throttle the account, and more than 30 days overdue we may suspend it. We will always email before either happens.

5. Service levels and support

Paid plans carry a 99.99% monthly uptime commitment measured per region, with service credits as the sole remedy for missing it. The measurement methodology and credit schedule are published in our documentation.

Scheduled maintenance is announced at least 72 hours in advance and is performed by draining regions gradually, so it should not interrupt live sessions. Emergency maintenance may happen without notice where it protects platform stability.

6. Your content and our rights

You retain all rights to the media, transcripts and metadata you send through the Service. We claim no ownership and we do not use customer media to train models.

You grant us the limited licence needed to operate the Service on your behalf: to ingest, transcode, cache, deliver, record and — where you enable those features — transcribe, translate and clip your media. That licence ends when the content is deleted.

7. Suspension and termination

You may close your account at any time from the dashboard. We delete media within 24 hours of deletion being requested, and remove backups within 30 days.

We may terminate for material breach of these terms, for non-payment after notice, or where continuing to serve an account would expose us to legal liability. Except in cases of unlawful content we will give 30 days’ notice and help you export your data.
