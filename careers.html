What you get

The specifics, not the perks

No table tennis. These are the things people told us actually changed their decision.

A four-day week after on-call

If you carry the pager for a week, the following Friday is yours. On-call is compensated separately, and the rotation never runs thinner than six people.

Remote across 14 countries

Hire-anywhere within four hours of CET, SGT or CST so a rotation always has daylight cover. Salary bands are location-adjusted and published internally.

Equity everyone can value

Options with a ten-year exercise window and a valuation we share every quarter. You will know what your grant is worth without having to ask.

Conference budget you keep

€3,000 a year for conferences, courses or hardware, with no approval chain. Unspent budget rolls over once, so a big year is possible.

Two on-sites a year

A whole-company week in spring and a team week in autumn, both fully covered. Attendance is expected; travelling with family is normal and paid for.

Ship in your first fortnight

Every new engineer puts something in production inside two weeks, paired with a buddy. If the onboarding blocks you, fixing it becomes your first task.

What to expect

Four steps, about two weeks

No take-home longer than three hours, and we pay for it. No whiteboard algorithms.

  1. 01

    Intro call

    Thirty minutes with the hiring manager. Mostly you asking us things.

  2. 02

    Technical conversation

    Ninety minutes on a system you have built and one you would build here.

  3. 03

    Paid exercise

    A real, scoped problem — three hours, €400, and you keep the code.

  4. 04

    Team and offer

    Meet the people you would work with, then a written offer within two days.
